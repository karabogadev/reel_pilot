Reel Pilot
GitHub

Reels that play themselves.

Reel Pilot adds the controls Instagram Reels is missing on the web. It skips ads, moves to the next reel when one ends, puts a seek bar on every video and remembers your playback speed.

Download for Brave and Chrome View the source

Free and open source. Works in any Chromium browser on desktop.

What the web player is missing

No more ads

Reels marked as ads are hidden and muted before they reach the screen, and the feed jumps straight past them. No more “Shop now” in the middle of your feed.

Next reel, automatically

When a reel ends, Reel Pilot scrolls to the next one. Want to watch it again first? Have each reel play up to five times before it moves on.

A seek bar you can drag

A thin progress bar runs along the bottom of every reel. Click or drag it to jump, hover to see the time. The arrow keys seek too.

Any speed from 0.25x to 4x

Speed up or slow down with S and D. The speed you pick carries over to every reel that follows, until you press R.

And a few more

  • FHold for double speed
  • ,.Step frame by frame
  • CCopy the frame as an image
  • []Loop part of a reel
  • KStay on this reel

A break reminder can also pause playback after a number of reels or minutes you choose, and wait until you continue.

Install in a minute

  1. Download and unzip

    Get reel-pilot.zip and unzip it somewhere you’ll keep it. The browser loads the extension from that folder.

  2. Turn on Developer mode

    Open brave://extensions or chrome://extensions and switch on Developer mode in the corner.

  3. Load the folder

    Click Load unpacked, pick the unzipped folder, then open instagram.com/reels.

The extension speaks English and Turkish. It follows your browser, and you can switch it in the popup.

It asks for one permission

  • Runs only on instagram.com, and stays off on Stories.
  • Keeps your settings in your browser’s extension storage. That is the one permission.
  • Sends nothing anywhere: no network requests, no analytics, no account.
  • Doesn’t rely on Instagram’s class names or selectors, so small redesigns are less likely to break it.

Reel Pilot is an independent project and is not affiliated with Instagram or Meta.
